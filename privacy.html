Privacy Policy — Alien Sergio

Alien Sergio, LLC  ·  Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. What This Covers

This policy describes what information Alien Sergio collects when you use the app, why we collect it, and what your options are. It should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

2. What We Collect

When you send a transmission, we collect:

  • Your question — whatever you write in the "Explain your situation" field
  • Your email address — required, used to deliver your reply and recover your transmission ID if you lose it
  • Your name — optional; if left blank, stored as "Anonymous"
  • Purchase details — which tier you selected, the amount paid, and an Apple transaction identifier used to confirm the purchase and prevent duplicate charges
  • Language — English or Spanish, so your reply comes back in the right one
  • Timestamps — when you sent the transmission and when it was answered

3. What We Don't Collect

We never see or store your payment card details. All payments are processed directly by Apple through In-App Purchase; Alien Sergio only receives confirmation that a purchase was made, not any payment information itself. We do not collect location data, and we do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

4. How We Use It

Your information is used only to:

  • Deliver a personal reply to your question
  • Email you a confirmation when your transmission is received, and a notice when your reply is ready
  • Recover your transmission ID by email, if you ask us to
  • Prevent the same purchase from being charged or processed twice
  • Respond appropriately if a message indicates you may be in crisis, as described in our Terms of Service

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service you paid for.

5. Who We Share It With

We use a small number of service providers to operate the app. Each only receives the information it needs to do its job:

We do not share your information with anyone else, and none of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

6. Where Your Data Lives

Our systems are hosted in the United States. If you are contacting Sergio from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. How Long We Keep It

We retain transmission records so you can retrieve your reply and so we can respond to support requests. If you'd like your data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove it, except where we are required to keep records for legal or accounting purposes.

8. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we have on file for your email address
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information

Reach us at support@aliensergio.app for any of these requests.

9. Children

Alien Sergio is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Security

Your information is stored on infrastructure that encrypts data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect what you share with us.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@aliensergio.app.
