Common Questions

I lost my transmission ID.
Open the app, go to "Check for contact," tap "Lost your ID?," and enter the email you used when you sent your transmission. We'll email your ID(s) back to you.

How long until I get a reply?
It depends on the tier you chose: Quick Signal within 48 hours, Deep Transmission in 3–5 days, First Contact in 5–6 days.

I want a refund.
Refunds are handled through Apple — go to reportaproblem.apple.com, or Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History on your iPhone.

I need to report something concerning.
Email us at the address above and we'll respond within 24 hours.
